# 08 — Stealth & Noise Management

**Status:** Established (design sessions 1–5)
**Depends on:** `01-core-resources.md` §3 (noise), §4 (heat)
**Depended on by:** `05-hacking-minigame.md` §4 (trace), `03-economy.md` (relay-hop sink)

Noise (`01` §3) is generated by acting; this toolbox is how a player *manages* it — cutting it after the fact, misattributing it, flattening its curve, decoying it, or transferring value without leaving a ledger trail. Stealth is the counterweight to the whole offensive kit: every loud tool in `06` has an answer here, at a price.

---

## 1. Tools (established)

| Tool | Function | Gate | Cost | Compute |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Log Scrubber** | Retroactively removes generated noise; tiered by how far back it reaches | Ethecoin | 20 / 55 / 140 EC | 5–12 |
| **Identity Spoofer** | Attributes noise to a fabricated handle; burns out after one session | Ethecoin | 35 EC | 6 |
| **Traffic Shaper** | Smooths the noise curve so the player never spikes above threshold, at the cost of a hard ceiling on action speed | Schematic | — | 10 |
| **Dead Drop** | Transfers loot or ethecoin without a traceable ledger entry | Reputation | — | 4 |

### Per-tool notes

**Log Scrubber** — the "undo" for noise, tiered by reach (recent → deep history) at 20/55/140 EC. Consumable EC-gated because it's a replaceable get-out-of-trouble button. The tiering is a sink escalator: cleaning a long loud session costs real money.

**Identity Spoofer** — doesn't remove noise, *reattributes* it to a fake handle, then burns out after one session. This is the "let someone else take the heat" play. Single-session burnout keeps it from being permanent cover — it's a per-op consumable of deception.

**Traffic Shaper** — the trade-off tool: never spike above threshold, but accept a **hard ceiling on action speed**. Schematic-gated because "never trip the trace" is a capability, not a consumable. It's how patient players run indefinitely-quiet at the cost of throughput — the inverse of the Overflow Kit's loud-and-fast (`06`).

**Dead Drop** — defeats the public ledger (`01` §2.2) for a single transfer. Reputation-gated because untraceable value movement distorts the investigator economy (evidence system, `12`). This is the laundering verb; without it, every EC move is followable.

## 2. Relay Chain — split gate

The onion-routing system. Gate is deliberately **split** (`02` §1.1):

- **Framework** → **schematic.** Unlocks the *ability* to route through intermediate nodes.
- **Additional hops** → **ethecoin, per session, ~8 EC each.**

Each hop reduces attribution and slows every action, and costs compute while active. The split means the *capability* is earned once but the *depth* is a recurring operational expense — a reliable ongoing EC sink (`03` §4). More hops = harder to trace = slower to act = more expensive: a clean three-way trade-off the player tunes per session by how hot the job is.

> **[PROPOSAL]** Per-hop effect, first pass: each hop adds a flat slice of trace-slowdown and a per-action latency tax, and reserves ~2–3 compute while active on top of the 8 EC/session. Diminishing returns past ~3 hops so stacking isn't a dominant always-on strategy. Numbers for playtest.

## 3. Ghost Protocol — the nuclear option

The fake-death mechanic, implemented as an **installable item**, not a scripted event.

- **Gate:** schematic, late-game.
- **Effect:** wipes **personal** heat entirely (server heat is unaffected — it's not yours to reset).
- **Cost:** the player's **handle, leaderboard position, and all reputation tied to that name.**

The most extreme heat-reduction option and **deliberately painful** — laying low (passive decay + self-mining income floor) is usually the better choice. Ghost Protocol is for when personal heat is so high the player is being hunted by name (named-hacker status) and no amount of lying low is fast enough. It is a *rebirth*, and it should feel like burning your life down to escape.

Interactions to preserve:

- Wiping reputation means forfeiting reputation-gated tools (`02` §2.3) until re-earned.
- The new identity starts cold — this is the intended escape from a named-hacker death spiral.
- Because it's an item (schematic-gated), *having the option* is itself a late-game progression marker.

> **[PROPOSAL]** Confirm whether Ghost Protocol severs item provenance (`../architecture/04-item-provenance.md`): does the reborn identity keep vault items? Cleanest answer for the anti-cheat model is **items stay** (they carry their own signed provenance chains and are owned by the DID, not the handle — `../architecture/02-identity-and-auth.md`), so Ghost Protocol resets *social/heat standing* but not *inventory*. This keeps it a heat-escape, not a duplication exploit. Flag for the federation-layer designer.

## 4. How the stealth kit maps to the offensive kit

| Problem created by... | Answered by... |
|---|---|
| Loud tools (Fuzzer, Overflow Kit) spiking noise | Traffic Shaper (prevent), Log Scrubber (undo) |
| Noise attributed to *you* | Identity Spoofer (misattribute), Mimic frame (decoy elsewhere, `10`) |
| Traceable EC/loot movement on the public ledger | Dead Drop |
| Attribution during a live breach | Relay Chain (slow the trace) |
| Personal heat already catastrophic | Ghost Protocol (reset identity) |

Every offensive escalation has a defensive-stealth counter at a compute/EC/reputation price. Balance the two columns together — buffing an offensive tool without checking its stealth counter can silently break the noise economy.
