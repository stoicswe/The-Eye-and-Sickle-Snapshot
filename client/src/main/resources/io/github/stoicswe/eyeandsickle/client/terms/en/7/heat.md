## DESCRIPTION

Heat is long-horizon. It accumulates from what you do to other people's
machines and it does not decay on its own the way noise does.

High heat changes who will deal with you, how often the Eye sweeps your
deployed miners, and eventually whether you are pursued by name. Some
contacts are only available while you are cold; some only while you are hot.

Defending your own rig never generates heat. Neither does self-mining. The
things that raise it are the things done outward.

The shape is not invented, though. Investigative attention really is
cumulative and really is sticky: it is far easier to attract than to shed,
it is allocated by priority rather than evenly, and being *individually*
interesting is categorically different from being one row in a dataset. The
distinction this game draws between noise (a moment) and heat (a reputation)
is the useful part to carry.
