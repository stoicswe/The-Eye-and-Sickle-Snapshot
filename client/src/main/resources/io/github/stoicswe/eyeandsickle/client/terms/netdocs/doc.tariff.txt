  BAND C   unmetered, per premises     flat
  BAND D   unmetered, per premises     flat, discretionary
  BAND E   not billed

Band E was created for premises whose meters could not be read, and was
intended to hold four addresses for one quarter. It held four hundred
and eleven for six years. Nobody was ever billed for them and nobody
was ever told they were not being billed, so nobody asked why a meter
that could not be read was still reporting.

The schedule that replaced this one has no Band E. The addresses were
not moved. They were removed.
