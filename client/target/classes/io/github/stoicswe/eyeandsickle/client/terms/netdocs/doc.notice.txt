Service will be interrupted between 02:00 and 04:00 in the second and
fourth week of each month for the foreseeable period. Interruptions are
routine. No action is required at any premises.

Residents reporting an interruption outside these hours are to be
advised that the schedule has been extended and that the extension is
routine. Residents reporting an interruption during these hours are to
be advised that the schedule is published.

The foreseeable period is not defined in this notice. It was not defined
in the notice this replaces, or in the one before that. The earliest
version held in the records office is dated eleven years ago and
describes the arrangement as temporary.
