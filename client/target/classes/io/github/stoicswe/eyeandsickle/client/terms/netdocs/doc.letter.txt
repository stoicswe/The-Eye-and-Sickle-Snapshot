The post is not what was advertised, and the advertisement was not a
mistake. Whoever wrote it knew what the post was and chose the words
that would fill it.

Six weeks now of reading counters that measure nothing anyone acts on,
and filing them where nobody reads them, and initialling a sheet to say
they were filed. The sheet is read. The sheet is the only thing that is
read.

There was a suggestion box in the corridor. It was removed during the
refurbishment and the wall was made good, and now there is no mark
where it was, which is a small thing and has been difficult to stop
thinking about.

It will not be sent. There is nowhere to send it that is not here.
