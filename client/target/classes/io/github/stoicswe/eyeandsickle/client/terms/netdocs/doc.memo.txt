An anomaly is a reading that cannot be explained by the equipment that
produced it. A reading that can be explained by the equipment is a
fault, and faults are reported through the maintenance channel.

Operators have been reporting faults as anomalies. The anomaly channel
is monitored; the maintenance channel is not, in the sense that
matters, because a fault is closed by fixing it and an anomaly is
closed by explaining it.

Explaining an anomaly reclassifies it as a fault. This is expected. A
month in which no anomaly is reclassified is a month in which the
channel was not used correctly.

There is no channel for a reading nobody can explain and nobody can
fix.
