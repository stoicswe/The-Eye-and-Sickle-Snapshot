FINDING. The reconciliation between the metered total and the billed
total has balanced to the unit in every period examined, across nine
periods, including two periods in which a substation was offline for
part of the month.

RISK. A reconciliation that never fails is not evidence that the two
figures agree. It is evidence that one of them is derived from the
other.

RECOMMENDATION. Establish which figure is measured and which is
computed, and from what.

MANAGEMENT RESPONSE. The reconciliation is performed by an established
procedure which has operated without exception since implementation. No
change proposed.

STATUS. Closed, management response accepted.
