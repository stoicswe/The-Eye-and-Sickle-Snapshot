Six entries, thirty minutes apart, identical to the character.

The log is handwritten. These entries are not. They were made with a
stamp — the same stamp, six times, with the clock advanced by hand
between impressions, which is visible in the ink where the wheel did
not seat.

Whoever did it was on shift. There is no version of that night in which
they were not there. They simply did not look at the west feed, and the
stamp was already in the drawer, which means they were not the first.
